## 1. GETTING STARTED

### How to Create Your Account

**Step 1 — Download LynkApp**
- Open the **Google Play Store** on your Android phone
- Search for **"LynkApp"**
- Tap **Install** → wait for it to download
- Tap **Open**

**Step 2 — Sign Up**

You have two options:

**Option A — Sign up with your email:**
1. Tap **"Create Account"**
2. Type your email address (e.g., yourname@gmail.com)
3. Create a password — make it at least 8 characters
4. Tap **"Sign Up"**
5. Check your email — you'll get a message from LynkApp
6. Open that email and tap **"Verify my email"**
7. Go back to the app and tap **"I've verified my email"**

**Option B — Sign up with Google (faster):**
1. Tap **"Continue with Google"**
2. Pick your Google account from the list
3. Done! You're in.

---

### Setting Up Your Profile (First Time)

After signing up, the app will walk you through a quick setup:

1. **Add a profile photo** — tap the camera circle → take a selfie or choose a photo from your phone
2. **Enter your name** — this is what others will see
3. **Choose a username** — like @yourname — this is your unique handle
4. **Write a short bio** — a sentence or two about yourself (optional)
5. **Pick your interests** — tap at least 3 topics you like (Music, Sports, Food, etc.)
6. Tap **"Get Started"** — you're in!

---

### How to Log In (Next Time)

1. Open LynkApp
2. Enter your email and password → tap **"Sign In"**
3. OR tap **"Continue with Google"** if you used Google to sign up

**Forgot your password?**
1. Tap **"Forgot Password"** on the login screen
2. Enter your email address
3. Check your email for a reset link
4. Tap the link → type a new password → log back in

## 2. YOUR HOME FEED

The **Feed** is the first thing you see when you open LynkApp. It shows posts from people you follow, trending content, and more.

### Reading Your Feed

- **Scroll down** to see more posts
- **Pull down from the top** to refresh and see the newest posts
- Posts can include text, photos, videos, or links

### Creating a Post

1. Tap the **➕ (plus button)** at the top or center of the screen
2. Choose what you want to share:
   - **Text** — just type what's on your mind
   - **Photo** — take a new photo or choose one from your gallery
   - **Video** — record or upload a short video
3. Add a caption if you want
4. Add a **#hashtag** (e.g., #foodie #weekend) so more people find your post
5. Tap **"Post"** — it's live!

### Reacting to Posts

| What you want to do | How to do it |
|---|---|
| ❤️ Like a post | Tap the heart icon |
| 💬 Comment | Tap the speech bubble icon → type your comment → tap Send |
| 🔁 Share | Tap the share icon → choose where to share |
| 🔖 Save | Tap the bookmark icon to save it for later |
| ••• More options | Tap the three dots on any post |

### Managing Your Own Posts

- Tap the **three dots (•••)** on one of your posts
- Options: **Edit**, **Delete**, **Change audience** (Public/Friends Only)

## 3. STORIES

Stories are photos or short videos that disappear after **24 hours**. They show up at the top of the Feed as small circles.

### Viewing Stories

1. Look at the top of your Feed — you'll see colorful circles with people's profile photos
2. Tap any circle to watch their story
3. Tap the **right side** of the screen to skip to the next story
4. Tap the **left side** to go back
5. Swipe down to close

### Creating Your Own Story

1. Tap the **"+"** on your profile circle at the top left, OR tap **"Create Story"**
2. Choose:
   - **📷 Camera** — take a photo right now
   - **🖼 Gallery** — pick a photo from your phone
   - **✏️ Text** — write a message on a colored background
3. Add stickers, text, or drawings if you want (optional)
4. Tap **"Share to Story"**

Your story will show for **24 hours** and then disappear automatically.

### Story Highlights

Want to keep a story forever? Save it as a Highlight:
1. View your own story
2. Tap **"Highlight"** at the bottom
3. Choose a highlight name (e.g., "Vacation", "My Dogs")
4. Highlights appear on your profile permanently

## 4. MESSAGES & DMs

Send private messages to anyone on LynkApp — just like texting.

### Opening Messages

- Tap the **💬 Messages icon** in the bottom navigation bar

### Starting a New Conversation

1. Tap the **✏️ pencil icon** (top right of Messages screen)
2. Search for a person by name or username
3. Tap their name
4. Type your message in the box at the bottom
5. Tap **Send** (the arrow button)

### What You Can Send

- 💬 Text messages
- 📷 Photos from your camera or gallery
- 🎥 Short videos
- 🎵 Voice messages — tap and hold the 🎤 microphone button
- 😂 GIFs — tap the GIF button
- 📍 Your location

### Reacting to Messages

- **Long-press** (hold your finger) on any message
- Pick a reaction emoji: ❤️ 😂 😮 👍 etc.

### Group Chats

1. Tap the **✏️ pencil icon** → **"New Group"**
2. Add people by searching their usernames
3. Give the group a name and photo
4. Tap **"Create Group"**

Everyone in the group can see all messages.

### Useful Message Features

| Feature | How |
|---|---|
| Delete a message | Long-press → tap "Delete" |
| Mark all as read | Tap ••• in Messages list → "Mark all read" |
| Archive a conversation | Swipe left on a chat → "Archive" |
| Mute notifications | Open chat → tap the 🔔 bell at the top |

## 5. YOUR PROFILE

Your profile is your personal page on LynkApp — like your own mini website.

### Viewing Your Profile

- Tap the **👤 Profile icon** in the bottom navigation bar

You'll see:
- Your profile photo and name
- Your bio
- How many **followers** and **following** you have
- All your posts in a grid

### Editing Your Profile

1. Tap **"Edit Profile"**
2. Change anything you want:
   - Profile photo
   - Cover photo (the banner at the top)
   - Display name
   - Username
   - Bio
   - Website link
   - Date of birth
3. Tap **"Save"**

### Profile Verification ✅

Verified users get a blue checkmark next to their name. To apply:
1. Go to your profile
2. Tap **"Apply for Verification"**
3. Fill in your information
4. LynkApp reviews your request (takes a few days)

### Profile Insights (See Who's Viewing You)

1. Tap **"Insights"** on your profile
2. See: profile views, post reach, who's visiting, best posting times

## 6. FRIENDS

Connect with people you know — or meet new people nearby.

### Adding Friends

**Option 1 — Search by name or username:**
1. Tap the **🔍 Search icon**
2. Type their name
3. Tap their profile → tap **"Add Friend"**

**Option 2 — Find Friends suggestions:**
1. Tap the **Friends icon** in the navigation
2. Tap **"Find Friends"**
3. Browse suggested people → tap **"Add Friend"** on anyone you know

**Option 3 — Nearby Friends:**
1. Go to Friends → tap **"Nearby"**
2. Allow location permission when asked
3. See who's using LynkApp near you

### Friend Requests

- When someone sends you a request, you'll get a notification
- Go to **Friends** → **"Requests"** to accept or decline

### Following vs. Friends

- **Follow** someone = you see their public posts (they don't have to follow back)
- **Friends** = you both follow each other (mutual connection)

## 7. GROUPS

Groups are communities around shared interests — like clubs you can join.

### Finding Groups to Join

1. Tap **Groups** in the navigation (or find it in the menu)
2. Browse **"Discover Groups"** or search for a topic
3. Tap any group → tap **"Join Group"**

### Creating Your Own Group

1. Tap **Groups** → **"Create Group"**
2. Fill in:
   - **Group name** (e.g., "DC Foodies 🍕")
   - **Description** — what's the group about?
   - **Privacy** — Public (anyone can join) or Private (invite only)
   - **Category** — Sports, Music, Food, etc.
3. Add a group photo
4. Tap **"Create"**

You're now the admin. You can invite people, post, and manage members.

### Posting in a Group

1. Go into the group
2. Tap **"Write something..."** at the top
3. Type your post, add photos if you want
4. Tap **"Post"**

### Group Chat

Each group has its own chat:
1. Go into the group
2. Tap the **"Chat"** tab
3. Message all members at once

## 9. NOTIFICATIONS

Stay up to date with everything happening around you.

### The Notifications Tab

- Tap the **🔔 bell icon** in the bottom navigation

You'll see notifications for:
- ❤️ Likes on your posts
- 💬 Comments on your posts
- 👤 New followers or friend requests
- 📩 New messages
- 🎉 Event reminders
- 🏆 Milestones (e.g., "You hit 100 followers!")

### Controlling What Notifications You Get

1. Go to **Settings** → **Notifications**
2. Turn on or off any category:
   - Likes
   - Comments
   - Messages
   - Events
   - Friends
   - News & Updates
3. Changes save automatically

### Quiet Hours (No Notifications While You Sleep)

1. Go to **Settings** → **Notifications** → **Quiet Hours**
2. Set your quiet time — e.g., 10:00 PM to 7:00 AM
3. Tap **"Save"**

During quiet hours, LynkApp won't send any push notifications to your phone.

## 11. DATING (OPTIONAL)

> 💕 The Dating section is completely optional. You have to turn it on yourself — it won't show up for people who don't want it.

### Setting Up Your Dating Profile

1. Tap **Dating** (heart icon in navigation)
2. Tap **"Set Up Dating Profile"**
3. Add:
   - 3–6 photos of yourself
   - A short bio (what are you looking for?)
   - Your age, height, and other details
   - What you're looking for (relationship, friendship, etc.)
4. Set your **preferences** — age range, distance, gender
5. Tap **"Start Discovering"**

### Swiping

- **Swipe RIGHT** (or tap the ✅ green heart) = You like them
- **Swipe LEFT** (or tap the ❌ red X) = Pass
- **Swipe UP** = Super Like (they'll know you really liked them!)

If you both swipe right on each other = **It's a Match! 🎉**

### After a Match

1. You'll get a celebration screen — congratulations!
2. Tap **"Send a Message"** to start chatting
3. Your matches appear in the **"Matches"** tab

### Speed Dating

Want to meet multiple people quickly?
1. Go to Dating → **"Speed Dating"**
2. Join a live session
3. You get short 1-on-1 video chats with potential matches
4. After each chat, you both decide: connect or pass

### Safety Center

Your safety is the priority:
- **Report** someone: tap ••• on their profile → "Report"
- **Block** someone: tap ••• → "Block"
- **Unmatch**: open the match → tap ••• → "Unmatch"
- **Safety tips**: Dating → "Safety Center" for advice and resources

## 12. LIVE STREAMING

Go live and share real-time video with your followers — or watch other people's live streams.

### Watching a Live Stream

1. Tap **Live** in the navigation (the red camera icon)
2. Browse live streams by category (Gaming, Music, Cooking, etc.)
3. Tap any stream to watch
4. Send a chat message or a gift 🎁 while watching

### Going Live Yourself

1. Tap **Live** → **"Go Live"**
2. Fill in:
   - **Stream title** (e.g., "Morning Cooking Show ☀️")
   - **Category** (what are you streaming?)
   - **Privacy** (Public, Followers Only, or Private)

### During Your Live Stream

- 🎁 **Receive gifts** — viewers can send virtual gifts
- 🤝 **Invite a co-host** — let a friend join your stream side by side
- 🔇 **Mute** — tap the mic icon to mute yourself
- 📸 **Flip camera** — switch between front and back camera

### Scheduling a Stream in Advance

1. Go to Live → **"Schedule"**
2. Pick a date, time, and add a description
3. Tap **"Schedule"**
4. Your followers can see it and tap "Remind Me"

## 13. MARKETPLACE — BUY & SELL

LynkApp has its own marketplace where you can buy and sell physical items — clothes, electronics, furniture, handmade goods, and more.

### Browsing the Marketplace

1. Tap **Marketplace** (shopping bag icon)
2. Browse by:
   - **Category** — Clothing, Electronics, Home & Garden, etc.
   - **Near Me** — items for sale close to you (map view available)
   - **Price range** — filter by budget
3. Tap any item to see full details, photos, price, and seller info

### Buying an Item

1. Open the item listing
2. Tap **"Buy Now"** or **"Add to Cart"**
3. Choose your **delivery method** — shipping OR local pickup
4. Enter your shipping address (if needed)
5. Pay with your card through secure checkout
6. You'll receive a confirmation and tracking info

### Selling an Item

1. Tap **"Sell"** (plus icon on the Marketplace page)
2. Fill in your listing:
   - **Title** — describe your item clearly
   - **Photos** — add 3–5 good photos (good lighting helps!)
   - **Price** — enter your asking price
   - **Category** — pick the right one
   - **Condition** — New / Like New / Good / Fair
   - **Description** — details about size, color, brand, etc.
   - **Location** — where pickup would be (if offering local pickup)
3. Tap **"Post Listing"**

Your item is now live! Interested buyers will message you.

### Seller Verification (KYC)

For larger transactions, LynkApp may ask you to verify your identity:
1. Go to Marketplace → **"Seller Dashboard"** → **"Verify Identity"**
2. Upload a photo of your ID
3. Takes 1–2 business days to approve

This protects both buyers and sellers.

## 15. VIDEO CALLS

Make free face-to-face video calls with friends — like FaceTime, but built right into LynkApp.

### Starting a Video Call with One Person

1. Open a conversation in **Messages**
2. Tap the **📹 camera icon** at the top right
3. Wait for them to pick up!

### During a Video Call

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| 🔇 Mute mic | Your voice is muted — they can't hear you |

### Group Video Calls

1. Go to a **Group Chat**
2. Tap the **camera icon** at the top
3. Everyone in the group gets an invitation to join
4. Up to multiple people can be on screen at once

### Meetings & Scheduled Calls

For more formal calls (work meetings, family gatherings):
1. Find **"Meetings"** in the menu
2. Tap **"Schedule a Meeting"**
3. Pick a date and time → invite specific people
4. They'll get a notification and can join at the scheduled time

## 16. SETTINGS & PRIVACY

### Getting to Settings

- Tap your **profile photo** at the top → **"Settings"**  
  OR  
- Tap the **☰ Menu** icon → **"Settings"**

---

### 🔒 Privacy Settings

**Who can see your posts?**
1. Settings → **Privacy** → **Account Privacy**
2. **Public** — anyone on LynkApp can see your posts
3. **Private** — only people you approve can see your posts

**Who can message you?**
1. Settings → **Privacy** → **Messages**
2. Choose: Everyone / Friends Only / Nobody

**Who can see your location?**
1. Settings → **Privacy** → **Location**
2. Turn location on/off, or set it to "While Using App"

---

### 🔐 Account Security

**Change your password:**
1. Settings → **Account Security** → **Change Password**
2. Enter your current password, then your new password
3. Tap **"Update Password"**

**Two-Factor Authentication (Extra security):**
1. Settings → **Account Security** → **Two-Factor Auth**
2. Turn it on → choose SMS or Authenticator App
3. From now on, logging in requires your password AND a code sent to your phone

**Log out of all devices:**
1. Settings → **Account Security** → **Active Sessions**
2. Tap **"Log Out All Devices"** — useful if you think someone else is on your account

---

### 🚫 Blocking & Reporting Someone

**Block someone:**
1. Go to their profile
2. Tap the **••• three dots** at the top right
3. Tap **"Block"**
4. They can no longer see your profile or message you

**Report someone:**
1. Go to their profile or tap ••• on their post/message
2. Tap **"Report"**
3. Choose a reason (Spam, Harassment, Fake Account, Inappropriate Content, etc.)
4. Our team reviews all reports within 24 hours

---

### 🎨 Appearance

**Dark Mode / Light Mode:**
1. Settings → **Appearance**
2. Choose **Dark**, **Light**, or **Auto** (follows your phone's setting)

**Text Size:**
1. Settings → **Appearance** → **Text Size**
2. Slide to make text bigger or smaller

---

### 🗑 Deleting Your Account

> ⚠️ This is permanent. All your posts, messages, and data will be deleted.

1. Settings → **Account** → **Delete Account**
2. Read the warning carefully
3. Enter your password to confirm
4. Tap **"Yes, Delete My Account"**

You have **30 days** to log back in and reactivate before it's permanently gone.

## 18. COMMON QUESTIONS (FAQ)

---

**❓ I forgot my password. What do I do?**  
On the login screen, tap **"Forgot Password"** → enter your email → check your email for a reset link.

---

**❓ Someone is harassing me. What can I do?**  
Go to their profile → tap **•••** → **"Block"** and/or **"Report"**. You can also go to Help & Support to report it directly to our team.

---

**❓ My post isn't getting any likes. Why?**  
Try adding hashtags, posting at different times, and engaging with others (like and comment on their posts). The more you interact, the more people see you.

---

**❓ Can I use LynkApp on my computer?**  
Yes! Visit **[your website URL]** in any web browser. The web version has most of the same features.

---

**❓ How do I turn off notifications?**  
Settings → **Notifications** → turn off any category you don't want. You can also set **Quiet Hours** so you're not disturbed at night.

---

**❓ Is LynkApp free?**  
Yes — LynkApp is completely free to download and use. Some optional features (like LynkApp Premium) offer extras for a small monthly fee.

---

**❓ How do I go private so strangers can't see my posts?**  
Settings → **Privacy** → **Account Privacy** → switch to **Private**. Now only people you approve as followers can see your content.

---

**❓ My app is running slow or crashing. What should I try?**  
1. Close the app completely and reopen it
2. Make sure you have a good Wi-Fi or cell signal
3. Check if there's a LynkApp update in the Play Store — update if there is
4. Restart your phone
5. If it's still happening, contact Support with a description of the issue

---

**❓ Can I recover deleted posts or messages?**  
Unfortunately, deleted posts and messages cannot be recovered. Before deleting, make sure you really want to!

---

**❓ How do I sell something in the Marketplace?**  
Tap **Marketplace** → tap **"Sell"** → fill in the details → tap **"Post Listing"**. See Section 13 for the full walkthrough.

---

**❓ What is a Verified badge?**  
The blue ✅ checkmark means LynkApp has confirmed that account is who they say they are. It helps you know you're talking to real people and real creators.

---

**❓ Is my personal information safe?**  
Yes. LynkApp encrypts your data, never sells your information to third parties, and gives you full control over your privacy settings. Read our full Privacy Policy in Settings → About → Privacy Policy.
