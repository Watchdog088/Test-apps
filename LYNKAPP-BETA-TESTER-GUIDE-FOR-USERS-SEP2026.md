# 🧪 LynkApp Beta Tester Guide
## For Regular Users — Step-by-Step Testing Walkthrough

**Version:** 1.0 — September 2026  
**Who this is for:** Anyone who has been invited to beta test LynkApp — no tech experience needed!  
**Time to complete:** You can do 1 session at a time — each takes about 15–30 minutes

---

> 🎉 **Thank you for being a beta tester!**  
> You've been chosen to try LynkApp before it launches to the public. Your job is simple: **use the app like a normal person and tell us when something doesn't work, feels confusing, or looks wrong.**  
> You're helping us make the app better for millions of future users. Every single piece of feedback matters!

---

## 📋 QUICK START — Read This First

### What is beta testing?
Beta testing means trying an app that isn't fully finished yet and reporting problems you find. Think of yourself as a quality checker — your job is to find things that are broken, confusing, or missing before regular customers see them.

### What you need
- ✅ An Android phone (or access to the LynkApp website on any browser)
- ✅ A working email address
- ✅ About 15–30 minutes per session
- ✅ This guide open on a second device or printed out

### How to report problems
Every time something goes wrong or confuses you, use **one of these methods:**

| Method | How |
|---|---|
| 🔴 **In-App Feedback Button** | Look for the floating pink "Feedback" button — tap it anytime |
| 📧 **Email** | Send details to: **support@lynkapp.com** |
| 📝 **Feedback Form** | The link will be sent to you with your beta invite |

### What to include in your report
When you report a problem, tell us:
1. **What you were trying to do** (e.g., "I was trying to post a photo")
2. **What happened instead** (e.g., "The app froze and went to a white screen")
3. **What phone/browser you're using** (e.g., "Samsung Galaxy S22, Chrome browser")
4. **A screenshot if possible** — hold the side button + volume down to take one

---

## ⚠️ KNOWN ISSUES (Don't Report These)

These are things we already know about and are working on:

| Issue | What Happens | What To Do Instead |
|---|---|---|
| Video calls may not connect | Calls may fail or show error | Test the chat features instead — calls are coming soon |
| Coin purchases are disabled | Buy Coins button may not work | This is intentional — skip the Wallet section for now |
| Some "Coming Soon" screens | A few pages show a placeholder | Just note it in your feedback and move on |
| Apple Sign-In button | May not work on Android | Use email or Google sign-in instead |
| Push notifications may be delayed | Notifications can be 1–5 minutes late | This is normal for beta — note the delay in feedback |

## 🗺️ TESTING SESSIONS — Your Step-by-Step Journey

Complete the sessions in order. You don't have to do them all in one day! Do 1–2 sessions per day and take breaks.

---

# SESSION 1: Getting Into the App
**Time:** 15–20 minutes  
**Goal:** Create your account and set up your profile

---

### ✅ STEP 1 — Download or Open LynkApp

**On Android phone:**
1. Open **Google Play Store**
2. Search for **"LynkApp"**
3. Tap **Install**
4. When done, tap **Open**

**On a computer or phone browser:**
1. Open your browser (Chrome, Safari, Firefox)
2. Go to the LynkApp website *(your beta invite email has the link)*
3. You'll see the LynkApp login screen

> 📝 **Report if:** The app doesn't install, won't open, or the website shows an error

---

### ✅ STEP 2 — Create Your Account

**Try signing up with email:**
1. Tap **"Create Account"** or **"Sign Up"**
2. Type your real email address
3. Create a password (at least 8 characters — mix letters and numbers)
4. Tap **"Sign Up"**
5. Open your email — look for a message from LynkApp
6. Open that email and tap **"Verify my email"**
7. Go back to the app and tap **"I've verified my email"**

> ⏱️ **Note:** The verification email should arrive within 2 minutes. If it takes longer, check your spam folder and report the delay.

> 📝 **Report if:** No email arrives after 5 minutes, the verify button doesn't work, or the app shows an error after verifying

---

### ✅ STEP 3 — Go Through the Welcome Setup

After signing up, the app will walk you through a setup screen:

1. **Add a profile photo** — tap the camera circle and take a selfie OR choose a photo
   > 📝 Report if: The photo upload fails or the photo looks blurry/stretched

2. **Enter your name** — type how you want others to see you
   > 📝 Report if: The name field doesn't accept spaces or special characters

3. **Choose a username** — like @yourname (must be unique)
   > 📝 Report if: It accepts a username that's already taken, or rejects a valid one

4. **Write a short bio** — just 1–2 sentences (optional)

5. **Pick your interests** — tap at least 3 topics you like
   > 📝 Report if: Tapping topics doesn't work or you can't get past this screen

6. Tap **"Get Started"**
   > 📝 Report if: Nothing happens when you tap, or the app crashes

---

### ✅ STEP 4 — Log Out and Log Back In

Let's make sure login works:
1. Go to **Settings** (tap the profile icon → gear icon)
2. Scroll down and tap **"Log Out"**
3. Tap **"Yes, log out"** to confirm
4. Now log back in with your email and password
5. Tap **"Sign In"**

> 📝 **Report if:** You can't log out, or you can't log back in with the correct password

---

**⭐ Session 1 Complete! Take a break if you need to. When you're ready, move to Session 2.**

---

---

# SESSION 2: The Home Feed
**Time:** 15–20 minutes  
**Goal:** Read posts, create your first post, and interact with content

---

### ✅ STEP 5 — Read Your Home Feed

1. After logging in, you should see the **Home Feed** — a list of posts
2. **Scroll down slowly** — let it load about 10–15 posts
3. Notice: Do the posts have photos, text, or videos?
4. **Pull down from the very top** to refresh the feed

> 📝 **Report if:** The feed is completely empty (no posts at all), it won't scroll, or you see error messages

---

### ✅ STEP 6 — Like, Comment, and Save a Post

Pick any post in your feed and test all of these:

**Like it:**
- Tap the ❤️ heart icon on any post
- The heart should turn red/filled
- Tap it again — the heart should go back to empty (unlike)

> 📝 Report if: The like doesn't register, or you can't unlike

**Comment on it:**
- Tap the 💬 speech bubble icon
- Type a short comment (e.g., "Great post!")
- Tap **Send**
- Check that your comment appears in the list

> 📝 Report if: The comment box won't open, or your comment doesn't show after sending

**Save it:**
- Tap the 🔖 bookmark icon on any post
- Tap the bookmark again to unsave it

> 📝 Report if: Saving doesn't seem to do anything

---

### ✅ STEP 7 — Create Your First Post

1. Tap the **➕ plus button** (usually at the top or center of the screen)
2. A "Create Post" screen should appear
3. Type something simple in the text box (e.g., "Hello LynkApp! Testing from beta 🎉")
4. Tap **"Post"**
5. Check that your post appears in the feed

> 📝 **Report if:** The plus button doesn't open anything, the Post button doesn't work, or your post doesn't appear

**Now try posting with a photo:**
1. Tap the **➕ plus button** again
2. This time, tap the photo/camera icon
3. Choose an existing photo from your gallery OR take a new one
4. Add a caption
5. Tap **"Post"**
6. Find your photo post in the feed

> 📝 **Report if:** Photo upload fails, photo looks wrong, or the post never appears

---

### ✅ STEP 8 — Share a Post

1. Find any post
2. Tap the **share icon** (looks like an arrow or curved arrow)
3. You should see options: Copy Link, Share to Feed, Share to Story, Share via...
4. Tap **"Copy Link"**

> 📝 **Report if:** No share options appear, or the share button doesn't respond

---

**⭐ Session 2 Complete!**

---

---

# SESSION 3: Stories
**Time:** 10–15 minutes  
**Goal:** Watch stories and create your own

---

### ✅ STEP 9 — Watch Someone's Story

1. Look at the **top of your Feed** — you should see small circles with people's photos
2. Tap any circle to watch their story
3. The story should play automatically
4. Tap the **right side** of the screen to skip to the next story
5. Tap the **left side** to go back one
6. Swipe **down** to close the story

> 📝 **Report if:** Stories don't load, tapping right/left doesn't work, or swiping down doesn't close them

---

### ✅ STEP 10 — Create Your Own Story

1. Tap the **"+" on your profile circle** at the top left of the Feed, OR tap **"Create Story"**
2. Choose one of:
   - **Take a photo now** with your camera
   - **Choose from gallery** — pick an existing photo
   - **Write text** — type on a colorful background

3. Once your story is ready, tap **"Share to Story"**
4. Your story should now appear at the top of the feed for others to see

> 📝 **Report if:** You can't create a story, the camera won't open, or your story doesn't appear after posting

---

**⭐ Session 3 Complete!**

# SESSION 4: Messages
**Time:** 15–20 minutes  
**Goal:** Send a message and test the chat features

---

> 💡 **Tip:** For this session, it helps to have a friend who is also a beta tester so you can message each other. If you don't, you can still test sending messages and observe what happens.

---

### ✅ STEP 11 — Open Messages and Start a Chat

1. Tap the **💬 Messages icon** in the bottom navigation bar
2. You should see your message inbox (it may be empty)
3. Tap the **✏️ pencil icon** (top right corner)
4. Search for another beta tester by their username
   - *(If you don't know anyone, try searching for the demo account name from your invite)*
5. Tap their name to open a new conversation
6. Type: **"Hello! Beta testing message 👋"**
7. Tap **Send**

> 📝 **Report if:** Messages won't open, you can't search for users, or the Send button doesn't work

---

### ✅ STEP 12 — Try Different Message Types

In the same conversation, try each of these:

**Send a photo:**
- Tap the 📷 camera/photo icon in the message bar
- Choose a photo from your gallery
- Tap Send
> 📝 Report if: Photo doesn't attach or doesn't send

**Send a voice message:**
- Tap and **hold** the 🎤 microphone icon
- Say something short ("Testing voice message!")
- Release to send
> 📝 Report if: The microphone doesn't activate or voice message doesn't play back

**React to a message:**
- Long-press (hold your finger) on any message
- Pick an emoji reaction (❤️ 😂 👍)
> 📝 Report if: Long press doesn't show reaction options

---

### ✅ STEP 13 — Create a Group Chat

1. From Messages, tap the **✏️ pencil icon**
2. Tap **"New Group"**
3. Add 2+ people (search by username)
4. Give the group a name (e.g., "Beta Test Squad")
5. Tap **"Create"**
6. Send a message in the group

> 📝 **Report if:** You can't create a group, members can't be found, or messages don't appear in the group

---

**⭐ Session 4 Complete!**

# SESSION 7: Notifications & Settings
**Time:** 10–15 minutes  
**Goal:** Check that notifications work and test your settings

---

### ✅ STEP 20 — Check Notifications

1. Tap the **🔔 bell icon** in the navigation
2. You should see notifications from your activity (likes, follows, messages)
3. Tap any notification — it should take you to the right place
4. Tap the back arrow to return

> 📝 **Report if:** Notifications are completely empty even though you've been active, or tapping a notification goes to the wrong page

---

### ✅ STEP 21 — Turn Off a Notification Type (Then Turn It Back On)

1. Go to **Settings** (tap your profile photo → gear icon)
2. Tap **"Notifications"**
3. Turn **OFF** the "Likes" toggle
4. Go back, like a post to trigger a notification
5. Check if you got a notification for that like (you shouldn't)
6. Go back to Settings → Notifications → turn **"Likes" back ON**

> 📝 **Report if:** The toggle doesn't save, or you still get notifications after turning them off

---

### ✅ STEP 22 — Check Privacy Settings

1. Go to **Settings** → **Privacy**
2. Find **"Account Privacy"**
3. Switch to **"Private"** — then switch back to **"Public"**
4. Find **"Who can message me"**
5. Change it to **"Friends Only"** — then change it back to **"Everyone"**

> 📝 **Report if:** Toggles don't work or don't save when you come back

---

### ✅ STEP 23 — Try Dark Mode

1. Go to **Settings** → **Appearance**
2. Tap **"Dark"** — the whole app should switch to a dark color scheme
3. Navigate around the app — does everything look right in dark mode?
4. Switch back to **"Light"** mode

> 📝 **Report if:** Dark mode makes text unreadable, buttons invisible, or anything looks broken
